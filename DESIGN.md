# Film Design & Audience Testing

## 1. Audience & Intent

- **Target Audience:** Family members, relatives, and peers who value family heritage, storytelling, and everyday cultural traditions.
- **Viewing Context:** Mobile screens (9:16 vertical) and desktop/TV (16:9 horizontal)[cite: 11, 18].
- **Intent:** Help viewers feel connected to family memory and understand the core lesson or proverb at the heart of the story by the end of the film[cite: 4, 6, 15].
